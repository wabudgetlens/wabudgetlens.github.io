About

A small project with a narrow job

WA Budget Lens exists to make Washington fiscal questions easier to examine without starting from a partisan conclusion.

The project focuses on spending, taxes, workload, financing, and measurable outcomes. The aim is to identify what changed, what drove the change, and what the public received in return.

Analysis is based on publicly available information, with a preference for legislative, agency, census, and other primary sources. Conclusions are updated when definitions change or better evidence becomes available.

Independence: WA Budget Lens is not affiliated with Washington State, any political party, campaign, candidate, or advocacy organization.

What this project is not

  • A campaign organization
  • A scorecard for parties or candidates
  • A claim that all spending growth is waste
  • A claim that all spending growth is justified
