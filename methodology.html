Methodology

Start with what actually moved

A budget number becomes useful only after its drivers, definitions, and constraints are made visible.

Five working buckets

BucketTypical question
Legal / constitutional coreWhat is the minimum compliant service level and delivery cost?
Entitlement / caseloadHow much change came from people served, utilization, rates, or eligibility?
Debt / contractWhat is already committed, and what can change only prospectively?
Discretionary programWhat outcome is being purchased, and is there duplication or a cheaper design?
Administration / deliveryCan the same service be delivered with lower cost, fewer errors, or less delay?

Accounting rules

  • A fund shift is not an efficiency saving unless total governmental cost falls.
  • Expected reversions should not be counted twice.
  • State-dollar reductions should show any federal match that disappears with them.
  • Recurring savings should not be booked until the operating change exists and can be measured.

Source discipline

Legislative budget documents, statutes, agency data, federal statistical sources, and official election materials are preferred. When fund definitions or category boundaries change, the comparison is labeled rather than silently treated as continuous.

Methodology evolves when the evidence requires it.
